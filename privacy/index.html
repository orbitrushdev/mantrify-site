Privacy policy

Updated November 24, 2026

The short version

Everything you do in Mantrify stays on your iPhone. The app hears you on the phone itself and never records your voice. The one thing it can send off the phone is an affirmation you wrote yourself, and only if you ask for it to be read aloud.

This policy covers the Mantrify app and this website.

What stays on your iPhone

Your affirmations, your practice and your settings are stored on your iPhone. That includes how many times you said each affirmation, your days and your streak, your reminder times, and the answers you gave when you first opened the app, such as your name and what you want to work on. We don’t get a copy, and the app doesn’t sync anything to iCloud. Backups of your iPhone include this data, as they do for most apps.

Widgets and the Lock Screen can show your affirmations, so anyone who looks at your phone may see them. If a practice stops without the app, for example after a crash, the Lock Screen hides its words after ten minutes.

Your voice

Mantrify uses the microphone to hear you say your affirmations and count them. Your speech is recognized on your iPhone. Your voice isn’t recorded and doesn’t leave the phone.

When you allow speech recognition, iOS shows Apple’s standard note that speech data may be sent to Apple. Mantrify asks only for recognition on the device, so nothing you say is sent to Apple.

Affirmations you write

You can write affirmations of your own. They are stored on your iPhone like everything else.

The voice reads your own words only if you agree. The first time you add one, you choose between “Add it and read it aloud” and “Add it without the voice”. You can change your answer at any time in Settings with “Read my own words too”. Turning it off also stops the voice reading recordings you already have.

When you agree, the app sends three things to our voice server, which runs on Supabase: the words, the voice you picked, and a random number the app made up for your phone. The server checks the words and passes them to ElevenLabs, the service that makes the recording. The recording comes back to your phone and stays there.

Our server doesn’t keep your words or the recordings. It keeps only counts of how much it has read each day and each month, so that nobody can use it up. When it turns a phrase down, it keeps a scrambled mark of the phrase for that day, which can’t be turned back into the words. Its log notes each answer without the words.

ElevenLabs files everything it makes in the history of the account our server uses. Our server deletes each item, words and recording, from that history right after making it, usually within a minute. ElevenLabs’ privacy policy covers its part.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. Their text can include your own affirmations, and nothing about them is sent anywhere.

Accounts, ads and analytics

Mantrify works without an account. It has no ads and no analytics, and it doesn’t track you across other apps or websites.

When you write to us

If you write to us by email, or with Report a problem in the app’s settings, we get your message and your email address. We use them only to answer you.

Crash reports

If you have chosen in your iPhone’s settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us reports of crashes in the app. Apple’s privacy policy covers them.

Links to other sites

Some links in the app open other websites, such as findahelpline.com on the support card. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

This website

This website has no cookies and no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security.

Deleting your data

To delete your data, delete the app. That removes your affirmations, your practice and any recordings from the phone. Our server keeps no words or recordings, and the counts it keeps can’t be linked to you once the app is gone, because the random number goes with it. A backup of your iPhone keeps its copy until that backup is deleted.

Changes

We’ll update this page when the app changes how it handles your data. The date at the top shows the last change.
