The short version

Mantrify is an iPhone app for saying affirmations out loud. Use it for your own practice, keep hate and threats out of your affirmations, and write to us when something goes wrong. The rest of this page says the same in more detail.

Who these terms are for

These terms cover the Mantrify app and this website. By using either one you agree to them. In the App Store, Apple’s standard licence agreement for apps applies as well.

A practice, with no promises

Mantrify helps you say your affirmations and counts them as you speak. What the practice brings is different for everyone, and we don’t promise a particular result.

Your own affirmations

You can write affirmations of your own. They stay on your phone. If you choose to have them read aloud, the app sends the words to our server and on to ElevenLabs, a voice service that makes the recording. Our privacy policy says exactly what is sent and what is kept.

The voices are made with AI

Every voice in Mantrify is generated with AI by ElevenLabs. That includes the recordings of words you wrote yourself.

What Mantrify won’t take

We don’t tolerate abusive or harmful content. You can’t add to your practice, or have the voice read:

  • anything sexual about a person under 18, or anything that puts a child at risk;
  • hate or attacks on people because of their race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, sex, gender, sexual orientation, disability or age;
  • threats, harassment or bullying;
  • sexually explicit content, or sexual content about a real person;
  • anything that encourages suicide, self-harm or an eating disorder, or explains how;
  • words meant to pass as a real person’s own, or to deceive someone;
  • anything illegal.

Because ElevenLabs makes the recordings, its Prohibited Use Policy applies to what you send as well.

A filter checks your words when you save them, and again before the voice reads them. A phrase that breaks these rules can’t be added to your practice. Words about wanting to die or hurting yourself aren’t saved either, and the app shows where to find help. If a phone keeps sending such phrases to our server, the voice stops reading new words from it until the next day, and we may turn it off for good.

Report a problem

If the voice reads something it shouldn’t, or anything else goes wrong, tell us: in the app under Settings, Report a problem, or by email at orbitrush@icloud.com. We read every report.

Your affirmations are yours. The app, its library of affirmations, its voices, sounds and design belong to us or are licensed to us. You may use them inside the app for your own practice. Please don’t copy them, resell them or take the recordings out of the app.

We work to keep Mantrify running well, and we provide it as it is: it can have errors and can be unavailable at times.
